i grew up in roseville, california, and now study data science at uc berkeley. i've always liked seeing a project go from an idea in a notebook to something another person can use.

i'm particularly interested in the overlap between applied machine learning, data-driven product decisions, and good engineering. some of my favorite projects have started with a small, annoyingly everyday problem — like deciding what to eat on campus — and turned into tools i actually use.

before berkeley, i spent a lot of time taking community college classes, building small projects, and refereeing soccer. these days, i'm usually learning something new, working with friends on a project, or finding an excuse to get outside.

if you're working on something interesting, [i'd love to hear about it](mailto:botu@berkeley.edu).
